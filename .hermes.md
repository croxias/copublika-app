## Governance

- Founder instructions and AL decisions must be distinguished from recommendations.
- Ale may perform reversible R1 development tasks within an explicitly approved scope.
- Architecture/scope decisions are R2 and require AL review.
- Costs, production deployment, secrets, sensitive data, destructive operations, and public launch are R3 and require Founder approval.
- Never infer approval from silence.

## Development workflow

1. Read `prd-copublika.md`, relevant docs, and nested `AGENTS.md` before editing.
2. Inspect Git status before work.
3. Use a feature branch; do not develop directly on `main`.
4. Keep secrets out of Git and Obsidian.
5. Run lint, typecheck, tests (when present), and build before claiming completion.
6. Do not push, deploy, merge, or change production without explicit authorization.
7. Record meaningful work, artifacts, verification, risks, and next gate in `/home/ubuntu/Croxias-Vault`.
